# Evidence

records/ contains real observations only and starts empty. Missing is not-tested. Templates stay in schemas/. Corrections supersede with reasons rather than erase history.

Source/replay/emulator observations cannot certify hardware. Physical verification requires independent review at exact revision. Expiration becomes stale on the board. New app/kernel/provider needs review or rerun.

Artifacts are sanitized, immutable or hash-pinned. Large media/traces stay out of git. Never publish personal routes.
