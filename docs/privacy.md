# Privacy

No telemetry or account service is provisioned. Precise location/history are sensitive; diagnostics exclude them by default and ask before export.

Tiles reveal viewed areas. Search/routing may reveal destinations/origins without a user ID. Identify recipients and retention; absence of an identifier is not location privacy.

Keep chosen/measured position separate. Show permission/revocation. Sharing one destination must not enable history/contact/continuous-position sync. Preview bounded handoff data and require explicit action.

Public fixtures use synthetic/public non-personal locations. Obtain consent and sanitize physical traces; endpoint removal alone may not prevent re-identification. Sensitive reports go privately.
