# Research lineage

The earlier research searched a cached builder catalog and inspected a smaller map-related subset. It was discovery, not exhaustive proof that nobody solved this. This public repository carries candidate sources and repeatable acceptance requirements; private research is not needed to understand a task or certify a device.

Earlier source/service probes motivate tests for route-profile discrimination, observation timestamps, accuracy provenance and source switching. Their current upstream behavior must be rerun before any defect claim. LOC-02 and ROUTE-01 own those reproductions and invalidation checks.

A source-distance branch examined earlier was dormant in the tested Linux path. Synthetic inputs do not establish outdoor error. Receiver scatter around its own mean measures repeatability, not absolute accuracy. GNSS bring-up did not prove screen-off application guidance.

Source URLs and the inspected Omarchy Mobile revisions are in data/sources.json. No device receives support credit from this research. Publish new sanitized, independently checked evidence here before certification.
